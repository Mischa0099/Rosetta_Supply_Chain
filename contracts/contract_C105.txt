SUPPLY AGREEMENT

Contract Reference: C105
Supplier: Delta Plastics Inc
Buyer: Adaptive Supply Chain Intelligence Corp ("Buyer")

1. TERM
This Agreement is effective as of December 23, 2024 ("Effective Date") and shall
remain in force for a period of 36 months, expiring on
December 23, 2027 ("Expiration Date"), unless terminated earlier in accordance
with the provisions herein.

2. SCOPE OF SUPPLY
The Supplier agrees to manufacture and deliver the following product categories to Buyer's
designated plants: rubber gaskets.

3. DELIVERY TERMS
All shipments under this Agreement shall be delivered DDP. The standard
manufacturing and shipment lead time shall not exceed 14 days from the date of
Buyer's purchase order confirmation.

4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
Supplier shall deliver all purchase orders within 10 days of the promised delivery date
communicated at order confirmation. Supplier shall maintain a minimum on-time delivery (OTD)
performance of 95% measured on a rolling quarterly basis, calculated as the number of
shipments delivered on or before the promised delivery date divided by total eligible shipments
in the period.

5. PRICING AND CURRENCY
All prices under this Agreement are denominated in INR and are subject to review on each
anniversary of the Effective Date.

6. PENALTIES
In the event Supplier fails to meet the SLA defined in Section 4 for any given calendar quarter,
Buyer may assess a service credit equal to 1% of the affected purchase order value.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws applicable to the Buyer's principal place of
business.

Signed,
Buyer Procurement Representative                    Supplier Authorized Representative
